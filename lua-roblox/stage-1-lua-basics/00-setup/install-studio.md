# Install Roblox Studio

Roblox Studio is free. It runs on Windows and macOS.

This guide walks you through installing it, signing in, and creating your first place.

---

## What You Need

- A computer running Windows 10 or newer, or macOS 10.13 or newer
- At least 4 GB of RAM
- 2 GB of free disk space
- An internet connection
- A Roblox account

If you do not have a Roblox account yet, you can create one during setup.

---

## Step 1 - Go to the Roblox Create Page

Open a browser and go to:

```text
https://create.roblox.com
```

This is the official Roblox developer site.
It is different from the main roblox.com player site.

On this page you will find:

- Download links for Roblox Studio
- Documentation
- Tutorials
- The Creator Dashboard

---

## Step 2 - Sign In

Click the **Sign In** button in the top right corner.

If you already have a Roblox account, enter your username and password.

If you do not have one, click **Sign Up** and create an account.
Use a real email address. You will need it later to publish games.

---

## Step 3 - Download Roblox Studio

After signing in, click the **Start Creating** button.

The site will detect your operating system and offer the correct download.

On Windows, you get a file called `RobloxStudioInstaller.exe`.

On macOS, you get `RobloxStudio.dmg`.

Click the download button and wait for the file to finish downloading.

---

## Step 4 - Install Roblox Studio

### On Windows

1. Open the downloaded `RobloxStudioInstaller.exe`
2. If Windows shows a security warning, click **More info** and then **Run anyway**
3. The installer will download the latest version
4. When it finishes, Roblox Studio opens automatically

### On macOS

1. Open the downloaded `RobloxStudio.dmg`
2. Drag the Roblox Studio icon into your Applications folder
3. Open Roblox Studio from Applications
4. If macOS warns about an unidentified developer, go to System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click **Open Anyway**

---

## Step 5 - Sign In Inside Studio

When Roblox Studio opens for the first time, it asks you to sign in.

Use the same account you used on the website.

After signing in, you will see the Studio start screen.

---

## Step 6 - Create Your First Place

On the start screen you will see several templates.

For this course, choose:

- **Baseplate**

This gives you a flat ground and a spawn point.
It is the perfect starting point for every lesson.

Give it a name like `Code Forge - Practice`.

Click **Create**.

---

## Step 7 - Explore the Interface

After the place opens, you will see:

- **Toolbar** at the top
- **Explorer** on the left
- **3D Viewport** in the center
- **Properties** on the right
- **Output** at the bottom (press F9 if you cannot see it)

Read the other setup guides to learn each panel:

- [explorer-guide.md](explorer-guide.md)
- [properties-guide.md](properties-guide.md)

---

## Step 8 - Run Your First Test

Press F5 on your keyboard.

The game starts in a test window.

You can move around with WASD and the mouse.

Press F8 to stop the test.

If everything worked, you are ready to move on.

---

## Saving Your Work

Roblox Studio saves automatically every few minutes.

But you should also save manually:

- Press Ctrl+S on Windows
- Press Command+S on macOS

The first time you save, Studio asks where to save the file.
Save it somewhere you will remember.

If you want to work on it from another computer later, use **File > Save to Roblox** instead.
That uploads it to your Roblox account.

---

## Common Problems

### The installer will not open on Windows

Right-click the installer and choose **Run as administrator**.

### Mac says the app is from an unidentified developer

Go to System Settings, then Privacy and Security.
Find the message about Roblox Studio and click **Open Anyway**.

### Studio asks for my password every time

Check the box that says **Remember me** on the sign-in screen.

### The 3D view is black or frozen

Your graphics driver may be out of date.
Update it from your computer manufacturer's website.

### F5 does not do anything

Click inside the 3D viewport first to make sure Studio has focus.
Then press F5 again.

### I cannot find the Output window

Press F9.
Or go to the **View** tab and click **Output**.
