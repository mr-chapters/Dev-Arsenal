
# 00 - Setup

Welcome to the first lesson.

Before writing any code, you need two things:

1. Roblox Studio installed
2. An understanding of the Studio interface

This lesson has four parts. Do them in order.

---

## Part 1 - Install Roblox Studio

See [install-studio.md](install-studio.md).

Time: 10 minutes.

You will:

- Download Roblox Studio
- Sign in with a Roblox account
- Create your first place

---

## Part 2 - The Explorer Panel

See [explorer-guide.md](explorer-guide.md).

Time: 10 minutes.

You will learn:

- What the Explorer is
- How objects are organized
- How to add and remove objects

The Explorer is the most important panel in Roblox Studio.
You will use it every single time you open the program.

---

## Part 3 - The Properties Panel

See [properties-guide.md](properties-guide.md).

Time: 10 minutes.

You will learn:

- What the Properties panel does
- How to change an object's settings
- The most important properties for beginners

The Properties panel is how you change anything in your game.
Position, color, size, behavior, all live here.

---

## Part 4 - Your First Script

After finishing the three guides above, come back here.

Open Roblox Studio.

Insert a Script into ServerScriptService.

Type this:

```lua
print("Setup complete")
```

Press F5 to play.

Look at the Output window.

If you see "Setup complete", you are ready for lesson 01.

## Common Problems

Problem: Roblox Studio will not install.

Solution: Make sure your computer meets the minimum requirements.
Check roblox.com for the latest version.

Problem: I cannot find the Output window.

Solution: Press F9. Or go to View tab and click Output.

Problem: My script does not print anything.

Solution: Make sure the Script is inside ServerScriptService.
Check the Output window for errors.

Problem: I do not know where ServerScriptService is.

Solution: Look at the Explorer panel on the left side of Studio.
It is one of the top-level items near the bottom.
